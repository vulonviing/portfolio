# Third-party audio

Piano playback uses a velocity-layer subset of **Salamander Grand Piano V3**,
recorded by Alexander Holm and distributed under the Creative Commons
Attribution 3.0 license (CC BY 3.0).

- Source: https://sfzinstruments.github.io/pianos/salamander/
- License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
- Packaged samples: browser-safe mono WAV derivatives of the velocity-8 sample layer

Solo violin playback uses the **Solo Violin / Arco Vib / p** subset of
**VSCO 2 Community Edition**, dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0.

- Source: https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE
- License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- Packaged samples: browser-safe mono WAV derivatives of the source WAV files
